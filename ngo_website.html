Welcome to Food for All Initiative

Our Mission

We are a Nairobi-based non-governmental organization dedicated to eradicating food insecurity. Our mission is to ensure that no individual or family goes to bed hungry by providing immediate relief and implementing sustainable agricultural programs within local communities.

Our Programmes

  • Community Feeding Centers
  • Urban Farming Training
  • School Breakfast Clubs
